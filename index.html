Questions

Before you book

Does acupressure hurt? +

No. Gentle pressure is applied at specific points, and most patients feel only mild pressure — not pain. Most people relax or even doze off during a session.

How many sessions will I need? +

It depends on the condition and how long you've had it. Many patients notice a difference within 3–4 sessions; a full course is usually assessed after your first visit.

How soon can I get an appointment? +

Most home visits can be scheduled within 24–48 hours of your WhatsApp message, subject to availability in your area.

Do you visit at home? +

Yes — every session is a home visit. There's no clinic to travel to; treatment happens in your own space, at a time that works for you.

What areas of Mumbai do you cover? +

All of Mumbai, appointment-based. Message on WhatsApp with your area and we'll confirm availability and timing.
